Privacy

Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice · Last updated May 2026

This website is the personal portfolio of baritone Luka Jozic. It is designed to share his artistic work and to make it easy to get in touch. Your privacy is respected: the site collects as little information as possible, and never sells or shares it for advertising.

01

Information We Collect

The only personal information this site handles is what you choose to send through the contact form — your name, e-mail address, and message. The form opens these details in your own e-mail application so you can send them directly; they are not stored on a database by this website.

02

How It Is Used

Information you send is used solely to read and reply to your message — for example, an enquiry about a performance, engagement, or collaboration. It is never used for automated marketing or shared with unrelated third parties.

03

Cookies & Analytics

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and does not build a profile of your browsing. Your browser may store small preferences — such as your chosen language (EN / SRB) — to improve your visit. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as a normal part of delivering those files.

04

External Links

The site links to external platforms such as Instagram and Operabase. Once you follow a link to another website, this notice no longer applies — those services are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

05

Your Rights

If you have been in contact and would like to know what correspondence is held, or wish to have it corrected or deleted, you may request this at any time. You can:

  • Request a copy of any message you have sent
  • Ask for corrections to any information held
  • Request that your correspondence be deleted
06

Contact

For any question about this notice or your information, please write to hello@lukajozic.com. This notice may be updated from time to time; the date above reflects the most recent revision.
